Squat Coach
Real-time squat form coaching
StatusStopped PlatformMobile
lilcoach.vercel.app (website)
▼

What I Built:

React + MediaPipe web app. Tracks 33 joints → computes angles → compares to a "normal range" built from 115 reps across 16 videos → plays voice coaching (42 pro-recorded clips). 12 fault types, rep counting, per-user calibration. Deployed; later extended to plank.

Why I Stopped:

1. Angle-only detection passed 95% of fake/cheated reps as valid; one camera can't catch all faults (knee-cave needs front, depth needs side)
2. Camera setup — plank needs a 1.5–2m side placement, impractical in a real gym
3. When AI drives code cost toward zero, the human checking output against reality becomes the bottleneck — and that checker was me

Market Analysis Findings:

1. Low TAM - Heavy users (20+ parcels/month) represent <5% of market
